How it works

  1. Describe your project and choose a package.
  2. Receive a personalized quote and file preparation instructions.
  3. Confirm scope, terms and payment arrangements.
  4. Receive your finished audio through an agreed delivery method.

What to prepare

Have your reference tracks, stem count and desired sound ready. For mastering, prepare a stereo mix; for mixing, prepare aligned individual tracks. Do not attach recordings to the initial inquiry. Private file delivery instructions follow project review.

Project terms

Included revisions apply to the agreed scope; extra work is quoted separately. Turnaround begins after the agreed payment and receipt of usable files. Stereo WAV and MP3 delivery is proposed as applicable; stems and project files are included only when specifically quoted. You must have the necessary rights to all materials you submit. Portfolio use requires your separate permission. The final quote will include delivery, cancellation, refund and file-retention terms for your review before payment. No order or payment is created by sending an inquiry.
